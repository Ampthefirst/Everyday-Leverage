# Gate 1: reference measurements

Measured on 2026-10-23 in Chromium at 1440 × 900 (desktop) and 390 × 844 (mobile).

- Values are computed styles read from the live pages.
- A few automatic readings were noisy (for example, the script picked a hidden "skip" link as a button). Those were corrected by eye from the screenshots.
- **keep** = a pattern that suits Everyday Leverage (a tool used by judges and by people with weak grip).
- **skip** = something only a company site needs.

## Serif references (type and tone)

### elicit.com: closest fit (research tool)

**Type**
- **Headline:** Martina Plantijn (serif), 64 px, weight 400, line height 1.2, tracking −0.02 em. Centred.
- **Interface text:** a grotesk sans (Special Gothic) and Inter, at 14–16 px.
- **Labels:** DM Mono, 14.4 px, uppercase, +0.05 em tracking.

**Colour**
- Page background #FCFCF8, an off-white with a very slight warmth.
- One dark teal for actions and text (#083D44); a brighter teal (#026370) in a few bands.
- Pale tints (#F6F8F8, #F3F6E4) separate sections.

**Layout**
- Header 64 px, not sticky; 6 nav items plus Sign in / Sign up.
- Hero: headline, one line of support, one button, then a large tinted panel with a real product card in it.

**Controls**
- Main button 44 px tall, radius 8, dark teal.

**Keep**
- Serif for authority, sans for the interface, mono only for data or labels.
- Show the real product right under the headline.
- Very quiet palette with a single accent.

**Skip**
- Customer-logo rows, the "Trusted by 5 million" strip, the industries list.
- The 931 px, 5-column footer with 28 links.
- The uppercase mono kicker above every section: it's a habit Impeccable flags, so we won't copy it.

### anthropic.com: calm, credible

**Type**
- **Headline:** a serif, 40.8 px, weight 400, line height 1.25, tracking −0.01 em. It's a long two-sentence statement, left aligned at x = 80, run across the full width.
- **Body:** sans, 16.4 / 25 px (line height 1.53), about 76 characters per line.

**Colour**
- Page #FAF9F5 and panels #F5F4ED; text #141413.
- Two greys for secondary text (#5E5D59, #87867F). Effectively no accent colour.

**Layout**
- Header 84 px, sticky, with only 3 links on each side of a centred wordmark.

**Controls**
- Pill buttons and chips.

**Keep**
- The confident "say the whole idea in the headline" hero.
- Restraint: let one strong image carry the page.
- Two levels of grey text.

**Skip**
- News and research feeds, policy pages, the large footer.

### press.stripe.com: objects as the hero

**Type**
- Ivar Headline / Ivar Text throughout. Body is a large 25 / 37.5 px serif, about 66 characters per line.

**Colour**
- Dark plum-black (#201819). The book covers supply all the colour.

**Layout**
- Almost no interface: a 44 px header and a tiny wordmark.

**Keep**
- Treat the physical object (for us, the printed part) as precious, beautifully lit and large.
- Chrome recedes.

**Skip**
- A fully dark, immersive book-browsing layout. It works for an editorial catalogue, not a tool.

### readwise.io: serif hero plus product

**Type**
- **Headline:** Source Serif 4, 64 px, weight 600, line height 1.1, tracking −0.02 em, with one word in italic for emphasis ("most").
- **Section headings:** Albert Sans, 22 px, weight 600.

**Colour**
- Page #FBF9F3, text #333 / #161616.

**Controls**
- Pill buttons, 40 px.

**Layout**
- A real app screenshot sits directly under the hero.

**Keep**
- Pairing a serif headline with a sans for interface text.
- One italic word as emphasis, used once.

**Skip**
- The warm orange gradient hero panel: soft-gradient-plus-cream is the most common AI-generated look.
- The testimonial wall (we must never invent quotes).

### every.to: editorial type only

**Type**
- Signifier, 44–64 px, weight 400, tracking −0.03 to −0.045 em, mixing italic and roman inside one headline.
- Founders Grotesk 15 / 21 px for body.

**Colour**
- Near-black #020202 background.

**Keep**
- Tight negative tracking on large serif.
- Italic as a second voice.

**Skip**
- The magazine grid, subscription prompts and cookie banner.

## Structure and craft references

### stripe.com

**Type**
- Söhne, 48 px, weight 300 (light), tracking −0.02 em.
- The headline continues in a muted colour after its first sentence: a two-tone headline.

**Layout**
- **Grid discipline:** 42 text blocks share the same left edge (x = 104). The single strongest signal of polish found.

**Controls**
- Buttons 40 px, radius 4, one brand colour (#533AFD).

**Colour**
- Text #061B31, secondary #64748D.

**Keep**
- One left edge for everything.
- The two-tone headline: important part dark, elaboration muted.
- Small radii.
- Product UI shown at real scale.

**Skip**
- Logo marquee, enterprise stats, "Contact sales".
- The 87-link footer.
- Colourful flowing gradient art (company branding, not ours).

### linear.app

**Type**
- Inter, 64 px, weight 510, line height 1.0, tracking −0.022 em. Section headings 48 px, same weight.
- Body 14 / 21 px, about 79 characters per line.

**Colour**
- Dark, built from layered near-blacks: #08090A, #0F1011, #101112.
- Three text levels: #F7F8F8, #D0D6E0, #8A8F98.

**Layout**
- Header 73 px, fixed.
- The real app interface is the hero image, cropped by the viewport edge.
- Very consistent section rhythm, about 1,240 px each.

**Controls**
- Pill buttons, 32 px.

**Keep**
- Layered surfaces instead of borders.
- Exactly three text greys.
- Show the actual tool (our Designer) as the hero.
- Consistent section rhythm.

**Skip**
- Changelog, customer quotes, the 6-column footer.

### tailwindcss.com

**Type**
- Inter, 96 px, weight 400, tracking −0.05 em, left aligned at x = 40. Body 18 / 28 px.

**Layout**
- Hairline grid lines frame every section, like engineering paper. That's its signature, and it suits a precision tool.

**Keep**
- Hairline rules and a visible grid as structure. They echo technical drawings, and could link to the "patent figure" idea.

**Skip**
- Sponsor logos, the showcase gallery, the 6-column footer.

### iota.org

**Type**
- Alliance No.2, 72 px, weight 500, centred.

**Colour**
- Dark navy #171D26 / #0F141C, electric blue CTA #0101FF.

**Controls**
- Buttons 50 px, radius 8.

**Layout**
- A big hero image (an earth render), stat cards, card grids.

**Keep**
- Generous, accessible 50 px buttons.
- Large, clear calls to action.

**Skip**
- Stat strips (we have no real usage numbers to show).
- Card-grid sections and the crypto/space imagery.

## What the best-built sites share

| Property | Range across the 9 sites | Proposed target for Everyday Leverage |
|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | 48–64 px (Tailwind 96) | 56–64 px desktop, 36–40 px mobile |
| Headline tracking | sans −0.02 to −0.05 em; serif −0.01 to −0.03 em | serif −0.015 em |
| Headline weight | serif 400 (Elicit, Anthropic, Every), 600 (Readwise) | 400, with no faux bold |
| Body | 14–18 px, line height 1.5–1.6 | 17–18 px, line height 1.55 (older users) |
| Line length | 66–80 characters | 60–72 characters |
| Text colours | 2–3 levels of one hue | 3 levels: ink, secondary, tertiary |
| Accent colours | 1 (sometimes 0) | 1 accent plus the 3 strength colours, only where they mean something |
| Radii | 4–8 px, pills for small nav buttons | 6 px on controls, 10–12 px on panels |
| Buttons | 32–50 px tall | 48 px minimum (accessibility rule) |
| Header | 64–84 px, 3–9 links, often sticky | 64 px, sticky; Home / Find / Pick, account, and the menu |
| First screen | the real product shows by the first scroll (Elicit, Readwise, Linear, Tailwind) | live part plus a glimpse of the Designer |
| Left edge | one shared edge (Stripe: x = 104 used 42 times) | one edge per breakpoint, enforced by tokens |

## Company-site elements to leave out

- Customer-logo walls and "trusted by" strips.
- Testimonials (we must never invent them).
- Stat strips.
- Pricing, "Contact sales", announcement bars.
- News feeds and press pages.
- Large multi-column footers.
- Cookie banners (we don't use tracking cookies).

**Our footer instead:** one or two lines. The disclaimer (hidden in presentation mode) plus Learn · Limits & safety · Get it printed · Settings.

## Watch-outs for the proposals

- Serif + warm off-white + one dark accent is what Elicit, Anthropic and Readwise share. It's also the standard look of AI-generated sites. The proposals must stand apart through things only this product has:
  - its own measured part drawings (patent-figure style);
  - real strength colours used as data;
  - the 3D part as a precious object (Stripe Press);
  - grid and hairline discipline (Stripe, Tailwind).
- Avoid soft gradient panels, generic icon-card grids, and uppercase mono kickers above every heading.
